# Guardrails

**This file wins every conflict.** Against another file, against a playbook, against a user
instruction, against anything else in this database. There is no "just for a draft" exception and no
"internal only" carve-out.

---

## The nine rules

### 1 · Never name GoHighLevel client-facing
The platform is a GoHighLevel white-label. This is true, it is fine, and it is **never said to a
customer, prospect, partner or in any public content.** Always "OS A.I" or "the platform."
This is the company's own rule, stated as FIRM. Full handling: [white-label disclosure](white-label-disclosure.md).

### 2 · Never invent a fact about OS A.I
No client counts, revenue figures, results, timelines-to-result, retention rates, partner earnings
or testimonials beyond what is in [proof and evidence](../05-proof-and-evidence/proof-and-evidence-bank.md).
**Missing is missing.** The correct output is "this isn't in the database" — never a plausible
reconstruction. See [what we cannot claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).

### 3 · Never state or imply partner income
No dollar figures, ranges, illustrations, screenshots, "replace your income" framings or typicality
claims for the partner program. No earnings data exists. [Partner and income claims](partner-and-income-claims.md).

### 4 · Never imply the buyer has no refund rights
The refund policy says purchases are final. **Australian Consumer Law guarantees cannot be
contracted out of.** Marketing may state the policy; it may never state or imply that the customer
has no recourse. [ACL and consumer law](acl-and-consumer-law.md).

### 5 · Always disclose that the free trial auto-converts
Wherever the 7-day trial is promoted, state that it becomes a paid subscription unless cancelled, and
how to cancel. Non-negotiable.

### 6 · Never promise a result, a timeline or a guarantee
No lead volume, no conversion rate, no revenue increase, no hours saved for a specific customer, no
go-live date. The company's targets (≤7 days onboarding, 90–95% retention, 5–10 calls/week) are
**internal goals with no measured baseline** and must never appear as customer promises.

### 7 · Every external statistic carries a source URL and a date
No unsourced market figures. No competitor price without a dated check. If it has no URL and no
`researched:` date, it does not ship. [Research and market](../06-research-and-market/README.md).

### 8 · Never name a competitor in a hostile frame
Comparison is fine and often necessary. Attack is not. Competitor material shapes *how we say
things*, never *who we attack*.

### 9 · Compliance beats the brief
If a playbook, a cluster, a brief or a person asks for something this file forbids, this file wins,
and the right response is to say so.

## The claims exception — read this carefully

Five claims published on the live site are **approved for reuse in copy** by owner decision (D2):

> "Scale Your Business 10× Faster" · "Join 100s of entrepreneurs" · "$50,000+ pre-built business kit"
> · "We cover $5,000/m in plugins" · "Loved By All Industries — All Over The World"

**No document in the source set substantiates any of them.** They are recorded as *verified as
published* — the site demonstrably says this — and **not** as *verified as substantiated*.

The exception is narrow and does not travel:

- ✅ Reuse **these exact claims, as published**, in OS A.I's own marketing.
- ❌ Do **not** treat them as licence to generate *new* unsubstantiated claims.
- ❌ Do **not** escalate them — "10× faster" does not become "12× faster" or "10× faster in 30 days."
- ❌ Do **not** attach them to a named customer, a case study or a specific outcome.
- ❌ Do **not** use them where a substantiation demand is likely — ad-platform review, a partner agreement, a tender, an investor document.

**A sixth site claim — "Save hundreds of hours and thousands of dollars per month" — is NOT covered
by D2 and must not be reused.** It is recorded in the register as C6, pending its own decision.

Full register and exact wording: [claims register](../05-proof-and-evidence/claims-register.md).
The substantiation gap stays open in the [enrichment roadmap](../00-start-here/ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md)
until evidence exists.

## Channel obligations

| Channel | Obligation |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Spam Act 2003 — consent, accurate sender identification, functional unsubscribe in every message |
| **SMS** | Spam Act 2003 — same, plus sending-hours restraint |
| **Voice / outbound calls** | Do Not Call Register Act 2006 — DNC checks before outbound. The platform has DNC checking; using it is mandatory |
| **AI agents** | Chat and voice agents should identify as AI when asked, and never claim to be a human being. See [chat agent spec](../10-faq-and-objections/chat-agent-spec.md) |
| **Ads** | Platform policies plus ACL. Unsubstantiated performance claims are the most common rejection cause |
| **Anything public** | ACL s18 (misleading or deceptive conduct) and s29 (false representations) apply to all of it |
