# Partner and income claims

> **Status: `draft`.** This file is the gate on partner marketing (gap G3). Until it is reviewed and
> moved to `approved`, **no partner-facing asset may be generated.** The rules below are the interim
> position and they are deliberately strict.

## The structure

Three-tier commissions: 40% (Pro) · 45% + 5% (Partner) · 50% + 30% + 10% (Platinum). Partners earn
on their own referrals and, at higher plans, on referrals made by partners they introduced. Detail
in [partner program](../02-offer-and-pricing/partner-program.md).

## Why this needs a compliance file at all

An ordinary affiliate program pays on your own referrals. This one pays on **your recruits' recruits**.
That is multi-level, and it is legal — but it moves the program inside a regulatory perimeter that a
flat affiliate scheme sits outside.

### Pyramid schemes — ACL Part 3-1 Div 3

Prohibited outright. Participation, promotion and inducement are all offences. The statutory test
turns on whether participants' payments are made in return for **the right to recruit others**
rather than for the supply of genuine goods or services.

**OS A.I is on the right side of that test:** there is a real platform, at a real price, that
customers buy and use whether or not they ever refer anyone. Commission is a term of a software
subscription, not the product.

**What could compromise that** is marketing, not structure. If recruitment becomes the pitch — if
assets sell "become a partner and earn" to people who have no interest in using the platform — the
program starts to *look like* the thing that is prohibited, regardless of how it is built.

### Referral selling — ACL s49

Prohibits inducing a purchase by representing that the buyer will receive a rebate, commission or
benefit **contingent on them supplying the names of other prospects** or otherwise helping the
seller make further sales, where that benefit depends on an event after the contract is made.

**Practical rule:** never close a sale with "and you can make it back by referring people."

### Earnings representations

Any statement, implication, illustration or screenshot suggesting what a partner could earn is a
claim requiring substantiation under s18 and s29. **No partner earnings data exists.** Therefore no
earnings representation of any kind can be made — not even a hypothetical, and not even one labelled
hypothetical.

## The interim rules

1. **No income figures.** Not stated, implied, ranged, averaged, illustrated, screenshotted or hypothesised.
2. **No "replace your income", "quit your job", "passive income", "financial freedom"** or equivalents.
3. **No typicality.** With no data there is no typical, and no basis for "results not typical" either.
4. **Recruitment is never the lead.** The product is the lead. Partnership is a term of the plan.
5. **Do not market partnership to general audiences.** It is for people already using the platform.
6. **Commission percentages may be stated** — they are contractual facts. **Never converted into dollars.**
7. **Partner-generated content is OS A.I's exposure.** Partners need a rules sheet before they are pointed at prospects.
8. **Any partner-facing asset needs founder sign-off** while this file is `draft`.

## What would move this to `approved`

- [ ] The Affiliate Policy read in full and mapped into a partner do/don't list
- [ ] A written statement of what a partner may and may not say when recruiting
- [ ] A disclosure standard for partner-generated content — how a partner identifies themselves as a partner
- [ ] A decision on whether partnership is marketed publicly at all, or only to existing customers
- [ ] Legal review of the three-tier structure against ACL Part 3-1 Div 3 — **this is the item worth paying for**
- [ ] Confirmation of who is accountable for partner conduct
