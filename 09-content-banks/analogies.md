# Bank — analogies

Ways to make an abstract argument land. **Use one per piece, maximum** — stacked metaphors read as
padding.

## For tool sprawl and duct tape

| Analogy | Makes the point that |
|---|---|
| **Eleven good tools is like eleven good musicians with no conductor.** Each one excellent, the result a noise | Individual quality doesn't produce a system |
| **Duct tape is a maintenance contract you didn't sign.** It holds until something moves | Connectors are ongoing infrastructure |
| **A connector is a handshake between two companies who've never met** and can each change their mind without telling you | Vendor dependency |
| **You've got eleven filing cabinets and the same customer in five of them** | No single record |

## For speed to lead

| Analogy | Makes the point that |
|---|---|
| **A lead going cold is a tap left running in a room you never go into** | Invisible loss |
| **Buying more leads to fix slow follow-up is filling a bath faster instead of putting the plug in** | The misdiagnosis |
| **The lead who went elsewhere doesn't send a complaint. They just stop existing** | No feedback signal |

## For CRM

| Analogy | Makes the point that |
|---|---|
| **A CRM that needs updating is a diary you have to remember to write in.** The ones that work write themselves | The design failure |
| **The spreadsheet won because it asks nothing of you** | Why the CRM was abandoned |

## For the founder bottleneck

| Analogy | Makes the point that |
|---|---|
| **You didn't become the bottleneck. The bottleneck formed around you** | Removes blame |
| **The business can't take a breath because it only has one set of lungs** | Key-person dependency |

## For automation and workflows

| Analogy | Makes the point that |
|---|---|
| **Automating an undefined process is paving a track people walk anyway** — you've made the wrong path permanent | Define before automating |
| **A workflow is a decision you make once instead of every Tuesday** | The core argument |
| **An automation nobody monitors is a smoke alarm with no battery** | Silent failure |

## For AI

| Analogy | Makes the point that |
|---|---|
| **An AI that can't see your calendar is a receptionist with no diary.** Polite, useless | Integration is the point |
| **You wouldn't hire someone and not tell them what the business does** — that's what an untrained agent is | Knowledge base matters |

## Rules

- **One per piece.** Stacked metaphors are a padding tell
- Concrete and physical. Taps, filing cabinets, smoke alarms — not "journeys" or "ecosystems"
- **If the analogy needs explaining, it has failed**
- Never a metaphor implying the reader is foolish
- Avoid war, sport and hustle metaphors — wrong register for this brand
