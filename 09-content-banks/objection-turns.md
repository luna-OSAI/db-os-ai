# Bank — objection turns

One-line turns, usable in copy or on a call. Full handling in
[10-faq-and-objections](../10-faq-and-objections/faq.md).

⚠️ `inferred` — these answer objections the team expects, not objections verified from real calls.

## The two real objections

Almost everything else is a proxy for one of these
([buyer psychology](../03-audience-and-icp/buyer-psychology.md)):

| Real objection | The turn |
|---|---|
| **"I don't believe this will actually get set up."** | That's why the setup is the product, not a bonus. Ten steps, named owners, a QA gate before anyone calls it done |
| **"I don't want to depend on something I don't understand."** | Then stay in the loop. Approval mode means nothing goes out without you seeing it |

**Answering the stated objection while leaving the real one alone is how deals stall.**

## Speed to lead

| Objection | Turn |
|---|---|
| "I respond quickly" | During business hours, probably. What about 7pm Friday? |
| "My leads aren't time-sensitive" | Being contacted first is a proxy for being taken seriously |
| "I'd rather reply personally" | Then let it hold the conversation until you can. The alternative is silence |

## AI and agents

| Objection | Turn |
|---|---|
| "I don't want a robot talking to my customers" | It's not a scripted bot. It's trained on your material, and you can approve every message before it sends |
| "It'll say something wrong" | Staged before it goes live, trained on your documents, and it escalates when someone's frustrated |
| "AI makes things up" | It answers from your material, not from the internet |
| "My customers want a person" | For the conversation that matters, yes. Not for "are you open Saturday?" |

## Tools and consolidation

| Objection | Turn |
|---|---|
| "My tools work fine" | Then this isn't for you. When did you last total what they cost? |
| "I don't want to migrate everything" | You don't. That's the setup |
| "All-in-one means mediocre at everything" | For a small team, a connected 8/10 beats five disconnected 10/10s |
| "One platform is one point of failure" | Against eleven points of failure and nothing that knows the whole customer |
| "I tried an all-in-one and never finished setting it up" | **The truest objection in the set.** Which is exactly why we do the setup |

## Automation

| Objection | Turn |
|---|---|
| "Automation feels impersonal" | Bad automation does. Automate the reply, not the relationship |
| "My business is too bespoke" | The bespoke part is. The reminders and confirmations aren't |
| "I don't have time to set it up" | That's the fee's entire reason for existing |
| "Zapier works fine" | Until a vendor changes a field. How would you find out? |

## CRM

| Objection | Turn |
|---|---|
| "Nobody uses our CRM" | Why would they? It asks for work and gives the benefit to someone else |
| "My spreadsheet works" | For you. What happens when someone else needs it? |
| "It'll be another thing nobody updates" | It stays current because the conversation and the booking already happen in it |

## Price

| Objection | Turn |
|---|---|
| "It's expensive" | Against HubSpot Professional at $890 plus $3,000 onboarding, it's a different conversation ⚠️ Tier 2 |
| "I could do this cheaper" | Probably. Cheaper platforms don't include the setup, which is where most of them fail |
| "What's the ROI?" | **Don't invent one.** No customer data exists. Answer with the comparison |
| "What do I get at $497 vs $97?" | **Not documented.** Say you'll confirm. Never guess — G12 |

## Trust and stage

| Objection | Turn |
|---|---|
| "How many clients do you have?" | We're early. I'd rather show you the system than quote a number |
| "Do you have case studies?" | Not published ones yet. Let me show you how the build actually works |
| "What results do clients get?" | **Describe the mechanism. Never a number** |
| "You're a small team" | Fair concern. Here's what's documented and who owns each step |

## Never use

- Any invented result, count or testimonial
- "Can you afford *not* to?"
- "You could make it back by referring people" — **ACL s49**
- Any income implication
- Guilt or manufactured urgency
