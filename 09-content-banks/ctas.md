# Bank — calls to action

**One CTA per asset.** Two asks is no ask.

## Live on the site

Verified from www.oscale.ai, 2026-08-18: **"Get Started" · "Start Free Trial" · "Message Oliver" /
"Message Olivia" · "Get Access Now"**.

⚠️ **"Start Free Trial" must always appear with the auto-conversion disclosure.**
[ACL and consumer law](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/acl-and-consumer-law.md).

## By awareness stage

**Cold / unaware — ask for nothing**

| CTA | Note |
|---|---|
| Check your own response time this week | Costs nothing, creates the realisation |
| Add up what you're paying for software | Same mechanism |
| Submit an enquiry through your own website and time the reply | **The strongest CTA in the bank** — the reader proves the problem to themselves |

**Problem-aware — low commitment**

| CTA | Note |
|---|---|
| Run the follow-up audit | Diagnostic lead magnet |
| Read the breakdown | Article → article |
| Get the checklist | Simple exchange |

**Solution-aware — a conversation**

| CTA | Note |
|---|---|
| Book a call | The default |
| Have a look at how it works | Demo, no pressure |
| Talk it through with Ariki | Named person converts better than a role |

**Ready — the transaction**

| CTA | Note |
|---|---|
| Start the trial | **Always with the auto-conversion disclosure** |
| Get started | Live site wording |
| See what the build involves | Routes to done-for-you |

## Rules

- **One per asset**
- Name the next step concretely. "Get started" is weaker than "book a 30-minute call"
- Match commitment to awareness. Asking a cold reader to book is why cold traffic does not convert
- For cold audiences, **the best CTA asks them to check something themselves** — no email, no commitment, and it produces the realisation
- Never stack a second ask in a P.S.

## Banned

- "Don't miss out", "act now", "limited spots"
- Countdown timers
- "Risk-free" — the trial converts and fees are non-refundable
- Any earnings implication — "start earning", "become a partner and earn"
- Guilt framing — "still losing leads?"
- "Book a call" to a cold audience with no prior value
