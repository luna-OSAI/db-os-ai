# Bank — hooks and openers

Each row: the line · when to use it · its pillar · its tier.

⚠️ **All rows are `inferred`** — written from the team's framing of the customer, not from customer
language. Once [voice of customer](../03-audience-and-icp/voice-of-customer.md) exists, this bank
gets rewritten with real phrasing, and it will get materially better.

## Problem-naming openers

| Line | Use when | Pillar | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| An enquiry lands at 7pm. You see it at 9am. | Cold, unaware. The strongest opener in the bank | 1 | inferred |
| The leads you're losing don't complain. | Awareness. Makes the invisible visible | 1 | inferred |
| You'll never know that deal was lost, or why. | Emotional register, still specific | 1 | inferred |
| Your automations are broken and nobody told you. | Duct-tape audience | 2 | inferred |
| Somebody in your business knows how it's all wired. One person. | Key-person risk | 2 | inferred |
| Your CRM has contacts in it from eighteen months ago. | CRM abandonment | 1 | inferred |
| You bought the platform. You never finished setting it up. | The most-felt line for this ICP | 3 | inferred |
| You started this for freedom and got a 60-hour week. | Founder bottleneck. Handle with care | 4 | inferred |
| Add up your software bill. Not the biggest one — all of them. | Sprawl | 2 | inferred |

## Contrarian openers — highest credibility

| Line | Use when | Pillar | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Most businesses don't need more leads.** | The flagship contrarian line | 1 | inferred |
| Your CRM isn't a discipline problem. | Removes blame, which is why it lands | 1 | inferred |
| Hiring moves the bottleneck. It doesn't remove it. | Argues against the obvious answer | 4 | inferred |
| Zapier isn't the problem. Maintaining Zapier is. | Concedes properly first | 2 | inferred |
| "AI for your business" is not a job. | Attacks category vagueness | 4 | inferred |
| Eleven good tools is still not a good system. | Concedes the tools are good | 2 | inferred |
| You can't automate a process you haven't defined. | Disqualifies usefully | 3 | inferred |
| Some things shouldn't be automated. | From a company selling automation. Very strong | 3 | inferred |

## Question openers — use sparingly

Rhetorical questions are banned as openers. **These are genuine diagnostic questions**, which is
different — they ask something the reader must actually answer.

| Line | Use when | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Could you take two weeks off? | Founder bottleneck | 4 |
| What happens to an enquiry that arrives on a Saturday? | Speed to lead | 1 |
| When did you last check that your automations are running? | Duct-tape | 2 |
| Who else could pick up your process tomorrow? | Workflows | 3 |

## Banned as openers

- "Tired of losing leads?" and every variant — **the most exhausted opener in the category**
- "In today's fast-paced business world"
- "We've all been there"
- "What if I told you"
- "Imagine a world where"
- Any dictionary definition
