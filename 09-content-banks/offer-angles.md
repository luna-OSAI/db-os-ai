# Bank — offer angles

Different ways into the same offer, by buyer and by pillar.

## For the SMB owner

| Angle | The line | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| **The after-hours angle** | Your enquiries don't stop at 5pm. Now neither does the reply | 1 |
| **The missed call** | Every missed call gets a text within seconds. That's the cheapest revenue you're not collecting | 1 |
| **The setup angle** | You don't have to learn it. We build it, you use it | 3 |
| **The consolidation angle** | One subscription instead of eleven. And one system that actually knows your customer | 2 |
| **The holiday test** | The business shouldn't stop when you do | 4 |
| **The honest angle** | If your follow-up is already tight, don't buy this | — |

## For the agency founder

## The routing rule

**Never present the subscription as the cheap version of the build.**
[Positioning](../04-voice-and-messaging/positioning.md) warns why: a prospect who needs the build but
talks themselves into $97 will never configure it, will churn, and will confirm the industry's worst
pattern.

**Route on "do you want to build it or have it built?"** — not on budget.

## Never

- Any tier difference not documented
- Any income or earnings framing
- "Risk-free" or "no risk"
- Any result promise
- Urgency or scarcity around pricing
