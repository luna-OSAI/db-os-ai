# Bank — subject lines

**Rules: 4–8 words. Sentence case. No colon-stacking. No fake "Re:". No emoji. No exclamation
marks.**

⚠️ All `inferred`. No open-rate data exists.

## Curiosity — grounded, not withheld

| Subject | Pillar |
|---|---|
| the leads that never complain | 1 |
| what happens at 7pm | 1 |
| your automations stopped four months ago | 2 |
| the eighteen-month-old contact list | 1 |
| nobody told you it broke | 2 |
| the $3,000 nobody mentions | 2 |

## Direct and functional

| Subject | Use |
|---|---|
| your follow-up audit | Lead magnet delivery |

| speed to lead, properly explained | Explainer |
| how to tell if it's connected | Diagnostic |

## Question — genuine, not rhetorical

| Subject | Pillar |
|---|---|
| could you take two weeks off | 4 |
| when did you last check | 2 |
| who else could do this | 3 |

## Re-engagement

| Subject | Note |
|---|---|
| still worth a look | No guilt |
| one thing worth checking | Give, don't ask |
| the audit, if you want it | Offer, no pressure |

**Never** "we miss you", "did you forget", "last chance", "are you still interested?"

## Banned

- ALL CAPS or Title Case
- Emoji, exclamation marks
- Fake "Re:" or "Fwd:"
- "Quick question"
- Countdowns, "final hours", "expires tonight"
- Any income or earnings implication
- Any performance claim — "how our clients doubled…"
- Personalisation gimmicks — "{{FirstName}}, this is for you"
