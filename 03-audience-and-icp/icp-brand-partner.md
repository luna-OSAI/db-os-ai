# ICP — the brand partner

> **`assumed` and gated.** No partner exists in any source document — no count, no profile, no
> earnings, no interview. This file is a working placeholder. **No partner-facing asset may be
> generated from it** until [partner and income claims](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/partner-and-income-claims.md)
> moves to `approved`. Gap G3.

## Who they probably are

Reasoned from the payout structure, not observed:

The tiers are named **Pro**, **Partner** and **Platinum**, and the payout ladder escalates far more
sharply than any documented feature difference. That points to a program designed for **existing
customers who resell** rather than for pure affiliates — most plausibly the
[agency founder](icp-agency-founder.md), who already sells systems to SMBs and can fold OS A.I into
an existing offer.

Likely segments, in order of plausibility:

1. **Agency owners and consultants** already selling to the same SMB market
2. **Existing OS A.I customers** who like it and know other owners
3. **Business coaches and community operators** with an audience of owners

## Why this file stays thin on purpose

The temptation with an empty ICP is to write a confident persona and let it harden into fact.
That is precisely the failure this database is built to prevent — and it is more dangerous here than
anywhere else, because partner marketing is the most legally exposed thing OS A.I does.

**A well-written but invented partner persona would produce well-written but invented partner copy**,
and partner copy is where an ACL problem actually costs money.

## What would make this real

- [ ] How many partners exist today
- [ ] Whether any are active, and what "active" means
- [ ] Whether partners are existing customers or recruited separately
- [ ] What partners actually do — refer, resell, or white-label
- [ ] What support and materials they receive
- [ ] Two conversations with real partners

## Interim rule

Where partnership must be mentioned to a general audience, treat it as **a term of the plan**, never
as an opportunity in its own right. The product is the pitch.
