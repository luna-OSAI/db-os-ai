# Playbook — video and VSL

## Purpose

YouTube is the education channel for both ICPs, and the marketing plan specifies **live demos and
system walkthroughs showing agents working in real use cases.**

**For a business with no case studies, a working demo is the strongest proof asset available** —
it is the one thing that cannot be faked and does not require a customer's permission.

## Formats

| Type | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-form** (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) | 30–60 sec | One idea. Hook in 3 seconds |
| **Education** (YouTube) | 6–12 min | One cluster argument, properly made |
| **Demo / walkthrough** | 8–15 min | **Show it working. The highest-value format** |
| **VSL** | 5–8 min | One offer, one action |
| **Founder talking-head** | 2–4 min | One argument, unscripted feel |

## Structure — education video

1. **Hook, 0–3 sec** — the problem or the contrarian claim. No intro, no logo sting
2. **The stakes, 3–30 sec** — why this costs money
3. **The argument** — the cluster's viewpoint
4. **The mechanism** — show it, do not describe it
5. **The honest limit** — what this does not solve
6. **One CTA**

## Structure — demo

1. **What you are about to see**, in one sentence
2. **The scenario** — a real situation, named
3. **Do it live.** Actually send the message. Actually let it book
4. **Show what happened in the CRM** — this is the part competitors skip and it is the most convincing part
5. **What it cannot do** — say it out loud
6. **One CTA**

## Do

- **Demo live wherever possible.** A recorded real interaction beats any claim
- Show the failure modes too — what happens when the agent does not know something. **This builds more trust than a flawless demo**
- Founder on camera. Tumai or Ariki, as themselves
- Australian English, natural speech
- Caption everything

## Don't

- **No fabricated dashboards, fake results or invented client data in a screen recording.** This is the highest fabrication risk in the entire database — a mocked-up "results" dashboard is a false representation under ACL s29
- **Never show the platform's underlying branding.** No GoHighLevel interface visible, no help-doc URLs, no login screens revealing the vendor
- No performance claim in the voiceover
- **No partner income framing**
- No countdown timers or fake scarcity in a VSL
- No stock-footage-and-inspirational-music opening

## Screen recording — the specific risk

Before publishing any screen recording:

- [ ] **No GoHighLevel branding, URL, help link or login screen visible anywhere in frame**
- [ ] No real customer data — names, emails, phone numbers
- [ ] No fabricated metrics, dashboards or result figures
- [ ] Test data clearly test data
- [ ] Nothing in a browser tab, bookmark bar or notification that reveals the vendor

**Watch the tab bar.** It is the most common way a white-label leaks.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Hook lands in 3 seconds
- [ ] One idea
- [ ] Mechanism shown, not just described
- [ ] Honest limit stated on camera
- [ ] **Screen recording checklist passed**
- [ ] No performance claim
- [ ] No partner income framing
- [ ] Banned language checked in the script
- [ ] Australian English
- [ ] Captioned
- [ ] One CTA
