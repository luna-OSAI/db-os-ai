# Playbook — sales conversations

## Purpose

The sales plan specifies a **diagnosis-first** approach: identify system failures rather than pitch
features. This playbook is that approach, made executable.

**The call is not a demo. It is a diagnosis.**

## Discovery call structure — 30–40 minutes

| Phase | Minutes | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | 2 | "I want to understand what's actually breaking before I show you anything" |
| **Current state** | 10 | How leads arrive, who responds, how fast, what happens after hours |
| **Find the leak** | 8 | Where enquiries die. Get them to say it, not you |
| **Quantify it** | 5 | Their numbers, not yours. "How many enquiries a week? How many convert?" |
| **Qualify or disqualify** | 3 | Against [disqualifiers](../03-audience-and-icp/disqualifiers.md). **Genuinely be willing to decline** |
| **Route** | 5 | Subscription or done-for-you — do they want to build it or have it built? |
| **Next step** | 3 | One clear action |

## The diagnostic questions

The ones that surface the leak:

1. "Walk me through what happens when an enquiry comes in at 7pm on a Friday."
2. "Who replies, and how do they know to reply?"
3. "How long, honestly, between enquiry and first response?"
4. "What happens if that person is on leave?"
5. "How many enquiries did you get last month? How many did you speak to?"
6. **"What's the last deal you lost that you think you should have won?"**
7. "What have you already tried?"
8. "If you took two weeks off, what stops?"

**Question 6 is the most valuable.** It usually produces the real story, in their words — and it is
also the fastest way to start filling [voice of customer](../03-audience-and-icp/voice-of-customer.md).

## Do

- **Diagnose before positioning.** Do not present until the problem is named by them
- **Route by build-vs-built**, not by budget. See [positioning](../04-voice-and-messaging/positioning.md) — never present the subscription as the cheap version of the build
- **Disqualify honestly.** A clean decline earns referrals
- **Record the call** (with consent) — it is the only route to closing the voice-of-customer gap
- Be honest about stage: "we're early, here's the system, judge it on that"

## Don't

- **Never invent a client count, result or case study.** This is where the pressure is highest and the rule is absolute
- Never guarantee a timeline or outcome
- **Never name GoHighLevel.** If asked what it is built on, use the answer in [white-label disclosure](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/white-label-disclosure.md)
- **Never state or imply partner income**
- Never claim a tier difference you cannot name
- Never say "it's easy"
- Never close with "you can make it back by referring people" — ACL s49

## Pre-call checklist

- [ ] ICP identified
- [ ] [What we cannot claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md) re-read
- [ ] Disqualifiers in mind
- [ ] Recording consent ready to request
- [ ] Both offer lines clear, and the routing question decided

## The three answers to have ready

| Asked | Say |
|---|---|
| "How many clients do you have?" | "We're early — I'd rather show you the system than quote a number." **Never invent one** |
| "Do you have case studies?" | "Not published ones yet. Let me walk you through exactly how the build works" |
| "What results do clients get?" | Describe the mechanism. **Never a number** |

**These will feel uncomfortable and they are the right answers.** An owner who has been sold to by
this category recognises invented numbers, and honesty here converts better than a fabricated
statistic that unravels in month two.
