# Playbook — lead magnets

## Purpose

Trade something genuinely useful for a contact detail, and **make the problem visible in the
process.** The marketing plan specifies "problem diagnostics" — audits and assessments that reveal
follow-up gaps rather than generic guides.

That is the right instinct: **a diagnostic that shows someone their own leak converts far better
than a PDF of tips**, because the reader generates the realisation themselves.

## Formats

| Type | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Diagnostic / audit** | 8–15 questions | **The best format for this business.** Reveals the leak |
| **Checklist** | 1–2 pages | Practical, immediately usable |
| **Framework / one-pager** | 1–2 pages | One mental model |
| **Swipe file** | 2–4 pages | Templates they can use today |
| **Guide** | 5–10 pages | Only where depth is genuinely needed |

## The rule that matters

**It must be useful even if they never buy.** A lead magnet that is a disguised sales pitch teaches
the reader that OS A.I's free material is not worth the email address — and that lesson is expensive
and permanent.

## Diagnostics — the strongest format

A good diagnostic:

1. Asks about their **current state**, not their aspirations
2. Produces a **specific finding**, not a score with no meaning
3. Names **what the finding costs**, without inventing a number
4. Suggests **one action** they could take without buying anything
5. Only then offers a conversation

**Candidates**, drawn from the clusters:

| Diagnostic | Cluster | Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| **The follow-up audit** | speed-to-lead | Response time, after-hours coverage, what happens on leave |
| **The stack audit** | software-sprawl | Total spend, overlaps, unused tools |
| **The connection check** | duct-taped | Which automations are actually running |
| **The bottleneck test** | founder-bottleneck | What stops when you do |

## Do

- Make the finding specific and about them
- Suggest an action they can take for free
- Keep it short. A one-page checklist beats a ten-page guide
- Deliver instantly
- Australian English

## Don't

- **No gated content that is really a brochure**
- No result claims in the finding — "you're losing $4,000 a month" is fabricated unless they supplied the numbers **and** the arithmetic is theirs
- No fake scoring that always produces a bad result
- **Never name GoHighLevel**
- No partner income implication

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Useful even if they never buy
- [ ] Produces a specific finding, not a vague score
- [ ] Any number is calculated from **their** inputs, and the arithmetic is shown
- [ ] Includes an action requiring no purchase
- [ ] No claim outside the register
- [ ] GoHighLevel not named
- [ ] Banned language Tiers 1–3 checked
- [ ] Australian English
- [ ] Delivery automation tested
