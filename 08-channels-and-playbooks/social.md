# Playbook — social

## Purpose

Put one argument in front of the ICP where they already are. **LinkedIn is primary** for both ICPs;
YouTube is the education channel; Facebook groups matter for the SMB owner.

Social does not close. It makes one point well enough to be remembered.

## Formats

| Format | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn — argument post** | 100–250 words | One idea. The default |
| **LinkedIn — story post** | 150–300 | A specific situation, one lesson |
| **LinkedIn — contrarian** | 100–200 | Argue against the obvious. **Highest engagement, highest credibility** |
| **Short-form video script** | 30–60 sec | One idea, hook in the first 3 seconds |
| **Facebook group comment** | 50–150 | Answer the question. **Never pitch** |

## The universal structure

1. **First line is the whole hook.** It is all that shows before "see more"
2. **White space.** One to two sentences per paragraph
3. **One idea**
4. **A specific detail** — a number with a source, or a concrete scenario
5. **Close on the idea**, not a CTA. Social CTAs suppress reach and read as selling

## Do

- Post as **Tumai or Ariki personally**, not as a brand page. Founder-led is the asset OS A.I has
- Open with the sharpest sentence in the piece
- Use the contrarian angles from [idea bank](../12-content-engine/idea-bank.md) — they perform and they build trust
- Attribute statistics, briefly but properly
- Answer comments as a person
- Australian English

## Don't

- No emoji-bulleted lists
- No "Agree?" or "Thoughts?" endings
- **No engagement bait** — "comment YES for the guide"
- No hook-and-withhold ("the third one shocked me")
- No exclamation marks
- **Never name GoHighLevel**
- No performance claim
- **No partner income implication** — this is where it is most tempting and most exposed
- Never pitch in someone else's group

## Pre-post checklist

- [ ] First line works alone
- [ ] One idea
- [ ] Under the word limit
- [ ] A specific detail, not just assertion
- [ ] Statistics attributed
- [ ] No claim outside the register
- [ ] GoHighLevel not named
- [ ] No partner income implication
- [ ] Banned language Tiers 1–3 checked
- [ ] Australian English, no emoji, no exclamation marks
- [ ] Posted from a person

## Worked example — LinkedIn contrarian post

> Most businesses I talk to don't need more leads.
>
> They need to answer the ones they already have.
>
> Harvard Business Review audited 2,241 companies in 2011. The average first response to a web
> enquiry took 42 hours. Twenty-three per cent never responded at all.
>
> Not "responded slowly". Never responded.
>
> The reason it keeps happening is that it's invisible. A lead who goes cold doesn't complain. They
> just go quiet, and a few weeks later you decide they weren't serious.
>
> So the month is quiet, and the conclusion is that you need more marketing.
>
> Try this: submit an enquiry through your own website tonight and time the reply.

**Why it works:** contrarian first line, one idea, sourced and dated, ends on an action the reader
does themselves rather than a CTA. No claim about OS A.I anywhere — and it is still unmistakably an
argument for what OS A.I sells.
