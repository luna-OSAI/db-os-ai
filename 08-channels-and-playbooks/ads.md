# Playbook — paid ads

## Purpose

Drive a specific person to a single-purpose page. The marketing plan is explicit: ads go to
**focused pages — audit, demo or system breakdown — not generic homepages.**

## Why ads carry the most claim risk

**Ad platforms enforce substantiation, and OS A.I cannot substantiate most performance claims.**
Meta, Google and LinkedIn all reject or restrict unsupported earnings and results claims — and the
partner program's commission structure makes "business opportunity" framing a rejection risk in its
own right.

The safe territory is **problem-led, capability-supported.** Name the problem, name the mechanism,
send them to a page.

## Formats

| Platform | Primary text | Headline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta** | 100–150 chars visible | 40 chars | Problem-led. Static or short video |
| **Google Search** | 90-char descriptions | 30-char headlines | Intent-led. Match the query |
| **LinkedIn** | 150 chars visible | 70 chars | The agency founder is here. More technical |

## The universal structure

1. **The problem, in their words** — one line
2. **The mechanism** — one line
3. **One CTA**

Three lines. That is the whole ad.

## Do

- One problem per ad
- Send to a **single-purpose page with no nav**
- Match ad language to page language exactly — mismatch destroys conversion and quality score
- Test the problem statement, not the adjectives
- Australian English

## Don't

- **No earnings or income claims of any kind** — instant rejection risk plus ACL exposure
- **No partner opportunity framing.** "Become a partner and earn" is a business-opportunity ad, and it is not permitted while [partner and income claims](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/partner-and-income-claims.md) is `draft`
- No performance claim about OS A.I
- **No "risk-free"** — the trial converts
- **No before/after implying a specific result**
- No competitor names in ad copy
- **Never name GoHighLevel**
- No countdowns or fake scarcity

## The registered claims in ads — extra caution

C1–C5 are approved for OS A.I's own marketing. **Ads are the one place to avoid them.** Ad platforms
demand substantiation on review, and an unsupported "10× Faster" or "Join 100s of entrepreneurs" is
a likely rejection and a possible account-level flag. [Guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md)
already excludes contexts where substantiation will be demanded — **this is that context.**

Use capability and problem framing instead. It performs better here anyway.

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] One problem, one mechanism, one CTA
- [ ] Destination is a single-purpose page
- [ ] Ad language matches page language
- [ ] **No earnings or income claim**
- [ ] **No partner opportunity framing**
- [ ] No performance claim
- [ ] No registered site claim used as the hook
- [ ] No "risk-free"; trial terms accurate
- [ ] No competitor named
- [ ] GoHighLevel not named
- [ ] Banned language Tiers 1–3 checked
- [ ] Australian English, no emoji, no exclamation marks
- [ ] **Tier 2 approval obtained**

## Worked example — Meta

> **Primary text:** An enquiry lands at 7pm. You see it at 9am. By then they've booked with whoever
> replied first — and you'll never know it happened.
>
> **Headline:** Answer enquiries at any hour
>
> **CTA:** Learn more → the speed-to-lead breakdown page

**Why it works:** specific problem, no claim about OS A.I, no number requiring substantiation, and
the CTA goes to an education page rather than a demo — which suits a cold, unaware audience.
