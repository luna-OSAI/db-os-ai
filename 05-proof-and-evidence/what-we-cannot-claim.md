# What we cannot claim

**The most important file in the proof layer.** Written down plainly so that six months from now no
AI fills the silence with something plausible.

An honest inventory of what OS A.I **does not have evidence for** is what stops a knowledge base
becoming a fabrication engine. When a needed fact is not here at any tier, the correct output is
**"this isn't in the database"** — not a reconstruction.

---

## These do not exist

### No customer results, of any kind
No lead volumes. No conversion rates. No revenue increases. No hours saved. No response-time
improvements. No before-and-after for any client.

### No case studies
Not one. No named client, no anonymised client, no sector example.

### No testimonials or reviews
No quotes. No G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Google reviews on file. No video testimonials.
**The site says "Loved By All Industries — All Over The World" and there is nothing behind it.**

### No customer count
**The site says "Join 100s of entrepreneurs."** No number appears in any document. Whether OS A.I
has 3 customers or 300 is not recorded anywhere in the source material.

### No revenue or MRR figure
$18K MRR is a **goal for end-2026**. $200K MRR is a **goal for end-2027**. Neither is a current
figure. Every "Current /m" field in the sales plan reads `[0000]`.

### No retention or churn data
90–95% retention is a target. No actual figure exists.

### No performance data of any kind
No campaign results. No CPL. No conversion rate. No traffic. The marketing plan's "Learnings" and
"Roadblocks" sections are blank — **no campaign has run and been measured.**

### No partner data
No partner count. No earnings distribution. No partner case study. No activity rate.

### No uptime, capacity or reliability figures
99.5% uptime is a **goal**. No measured availability, no concurrency limit, no throughput data.

### No security or compliance certification
No SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR attestation or penetration test on file. **Never imply any of it.**

### No awards, accreditations or media coverage
None recorded.

### No competitor pricing that has been verified
The "$890/month HubSpot" figure appears repeatedly with no source and no date.

### No tier differentiation
What Platinum gives you that Pro does not — beyond commission percentage — is not documented.

### No voice of customer
Zero sentences of real prospect language. See [voice of customer](../03-audience-and-icp/voice-of-customer.md).

## What to say instead

| Asked | Say |
|---|---|
| "How many clients do you have?" | "We're early — I'd rather show you the system than quote a number." **Do not invent one.** |
| "Do you have case studies?" | "Not published ones yet. I can walk you through exactly how the build works." |
| "What results do clients get?" | Describe the **mechanism**, not an outcome. "The agent responds inside a minute, any hour" is true. "Clients see 40% more bookings" is not. |
| "How long until it's live?" | "We target under a week. I won't promise a date until I've seen your setup." |
| "Is it secure?" | "That's a question I'll get you a proper answer to" — then get one. **Never improvise.** |
| "What's your uptime?" | Same. Do not quote the goal. |

---

## Why saying this well is an asset

**Early-stage is not a weakness to hide — it is a position to hold.** An owner who has been sold to
by every SaaS company in the category will recognise the difference between someone quoting invented
numbers and someone saying "we're early, here's the system, judge it on that."

The businesses that get into trouble are the ones that fill the gap. OS A.I competes on **the
argument and the mechanism**, both of which are strong and both of which are true.

## The cheapest fixes

Ranked by leverage — full detail in the [enrichment roadmap](../00-start-here/ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md):

1. **One customer count.** Substantiates C2 immediately.
2. **Three testimonials with written permission.** Substantiates C5.
3. **Record five sales calls.** Closes the voice-of-customer gap and upgrades both ICPs.
4. **One documented client result, with permission.** Unlocks the entire performance-claim category.
