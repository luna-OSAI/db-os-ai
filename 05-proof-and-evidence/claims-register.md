# Claims register

**The single home for every claim OS A.I is permitted to make.** If a claim is not here, it does not
ship. New claims are added by decision, not by drafting.

## How to read the two columns

- **`confidence`** — can we trace this to a source? *Verified* means the source exists and says this.
- **`substantiated`** — is there evidence the claim is **true**?

A claim can be `verified: yes` / `substantiated: no`. That means **the brand demonstrably says this,
and no evidence has been produced for it.** Per owner decision D2, those claims remain usable in
OS A.I's own marketing.

| C6 | "Save hundreds of hours and thousands of dollars per month" | ✅ live site | ❌ no measurement on file |

**Bounds — these do not travel:**

- ✅ Reuse **as written**, in OS A.I marketing
- ❌ Never **escalate** — "10× faster" does not become "12×", or "10× faster in 30 days"
- ❌ Never **attach to a named customer**, case study or specific outcome
- ❌ Never use as **licence to generate new** unsubstantiated claims
- ❌ Never use where **substantiation will be demanded** — ad-platform review, partner agreements, tenders, investor materials

The substantiation gap stays open in the [enrichment roadmap](../00-start-here/ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md)
until evidence exists. Closing it is cheap: a customer count, a plugin cost breakdown, a kit
valuation and five reviews would substantiate four of the six.

## Adding a claim

1. Identify its type — capability, commercial, market or performance ([claims policy](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/claims-policy.md))
2. Name the source, with a date and a URL where external
3. Set `verified` and `substantiated` separately and honestly
4. Get Tier 2 sign-off ([approval rules](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/approval-rules.md))
5. Add it here **before** it appears in an asset
