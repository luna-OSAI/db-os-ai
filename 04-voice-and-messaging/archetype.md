# Brand archetype — Magician 70 / Ruler 30

## The decision

OS A.I is **70% Magician, 30% Ruler.** This is a company decision with a documented rationale, and
the tagline **"The Magic Is In The System"** encodes it precisely: the wonder (Magician) lives in
the infrastructure (Ruler).

> **Note on sourcing.** The archetype *choice* is verified — the company made it. The archetype
> *theory* around it is external brand research (Jung; Mark & Pearson; Moore & Gillette) and is
> cited as such, not as a fact about OS A.I.

## Magician — the 70%

| | |
|---|---|
| Core desire | Transformation — making the impossible real |
| Core fear | Unintended consequences; that the power backfires |
| Role | **The guide, not the hero.** The customer is the hero |
| Promise | "Through us, what you want becomes possible" |
| **Shadow** | **The snake-oil merchant.** Manipulation, hype, overpromise |

**The shadow is the operative part for OS A.I.** The Magician's failure mode is *literally* the con
artist — a brand selling transformation with nothing underneath it. For a company with no case
studies yet, that risk is not theoretical.

**The antidote is the Ruler 30%.** Specificity, process, named mechanism, visible competence. Every
transformation claim earns its keep by being followed with how.

## Ruler — the 30%

| | |
|---|---|
| Core desire | Control — order and stability |
| Core fear | Chaos |
| Role | The authority who makes things reliable |
| Contribution | Structure, precision, competence, premium posture |

The Ruler is why "ten steps, named owners, a QA gate" is on-brand and why a feature dump is not.
Ruler is *demonstrated order*, not *listed capability*.

## The blend, operationally

| Where | Magician | Ruler |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | ✅ dominant | |
| Subhead | ✅ | ✅ |
| Body copy | | ✅ dominant |
| Proof and process | | ✅ dominant |
| CTA | ✅ | |
| Objection handling | | ✅ dominant |

**All Magician reads as hype. All Ruler reads as a manual.** The failure mode of most OS A.I copy
will be Magician drift — because transformation language is easier to write than mechanism.

## Language the archetype licenses

**Transformation:** transform · turn X into Y · shift · what becomes possible
**Vision:** imagine · what if the business ran without · picture
**System (the Ruler half):** built · installed · configured · runs · handles · routes

**Explicitly not licensed**, despite appearing in the archetype research: *alchemy · enchantment ·
spellbinding · mesmerising · luminous · unleash · ignite.* The theory recommends them; they violate
the no-BS half of the voice and are banned in [banned language](banned-language.md). **Where the
archetype research and the brand voice conflict, brand voice wins** — the research is external
theory, the voice is the company's own decision.

## The visual note

The archetype document prescribes purple, indigo, black, gold and silver. **Whether OS A.I actually
uses any of this is unknown** — no logo, palette or type specification was supplied (gap G8). Do not
treat the archetype palette as the brand palette.
