# Cluster — cost savings

**Pillar 2.** The most claim-sensitive cluster in the set. Every number here needs a source, and the
temptation to round in OS A.I's favour is highest.

## 1 · The customer pain

The software bill grew without a decision ever being made about it. Each subscription was small
enough to approve without thinking. Together they are one of the larger line items in the business,
and nobody has ever sat down and totalled them.

Worse: the pricing models punish exactly what the owner is trying to do. Seat-based pricing charges
more when the team grows. Contact-tier pricing charges more when the list grows. **The tools get
more expensive precisely as the business succeeds.**

## 2 · OS A.I's viewpoint

**Consolidation is arithmetic, not a feeling — so do the arithmetic honestly.**

1. Compare like with like. HubSpot's free tier is free; the comparison that matters is the tier where a business actually gets automation.
2. **Include setup costs.** HubSpot Professional carries a mandatory $3,000 onboarding fee. That is not a footnote — it is the argument.
3. Include the cost of the seams: the hours moving data, the breakage, the person maintaining it.
4. Include the tools being paid for and not used — 25–30% of licences, industry-wide.
5. Then let the reader do the sum themselves. **A total you calculated for them is a sales claim. A total they calculated is a decision.**

**What the brand refuses to say:** that OS A.I will save a specific business a specific amount.
There is no data for that. The claim is that the comparison is worth making, not that the outcome is
guaranteed.

## 3 · The solution

- **Three tiers: $97 / $297 / $497 per month** — [subscription plans](../02-offer-and-pricing/subscription-plans.md)
- **No seat-based pricing** documented — growth does not automatically raise the bill
- **One subscription replacing ten categories** — [what we replace](../02-offer-and-pricing/what-we-replace.md)
- **Setup available as a defined cost** rather than a hidden project — [done-for-you](../02-offer-and-pricing/done-for-you-services.md)

⚠️ **What is NOT documented: what differs between the three tiers** beyond partner payout (gap G12).
Never invent a tier difference in cost-comparison copy.

## 5 · Common objections

| Objection | The turn |
|---|---|
| "It's cheaper to keep what I've got" | Possibly. Add it up including the tools you're not using, then decide |
| "$497 is a lot for software" | Against HubSpot Professional at $890 plus $3,000 onboarding, it's a different conversation. Name the tier |
| "There are cheaper all-in-ones" | Yes. Cheaper platforms don't include the setup, which is where most of them fail |
| "What's the ROI?" | **Do not invent one.** No customer data exists. Answer with the comparison, not a projection |
| "What do I get at $497 that I don't at $97?" | **Not documented.** Say you'll confirm it. Do not guess — G12 |
