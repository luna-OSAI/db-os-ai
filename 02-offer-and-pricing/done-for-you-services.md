## The four builds

| Product | Setup | Monthly | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pro Chat Agent** | $2,500 | $297 | A chat agent built for the client's business — qualifies leads, answers questions, books appointments 24/7. Integrated with existing systems, conversation flows built around their model, ongoing optimisation |
| **Platinum Chat Agent** | $6,800 | $497 | Multi-channel (website, social, SMS), advanced lead scoring and routing, custom reporting dashboards, priority support with dedicated account management |
| **Pro Voice Agent** | $5,500 | $297 | Human-like voice agent handling inbound and outbound calls — qualification, booking, support |
| **Platinum Voice Agent** | $11,100 | $497 | Adds advanced NLP, sentiment analysis, CRM integration with call recording and transcription, custom voice training for industry terminology |

**Voice costs roughly twice chat at the same tier.** That is the pricing logic and it is defensible:
voice is harder to build, harder to tune, and carries call-compliance obligations chat does not.

## Why the setup fee exists

This is the objection that decides the sale, so the answer needs to be solid.

The setup fee buys the **ten-step onboarding process** in [how we work](../01-company/how-we-work.md):
workspace creation, CRM personalisation in two stages, a kickoff call prepared against the client's
own top three problems, lead-source connection, automation activation, and a QA gate before anyone
calls it done.

The honest framing: **the software is not the hard part — the setup is.** Most SMBs who buy an
all-in-one platform never finish configuring it, and an unconfigured platform is worse than the
tools it replaced. The setup fee is what makes the subscription actually work.

That argument is also the bridge between the two revenue lines, and it is the strongest thing OS A.I
can say about why it is not just reselling software.

## Writing about done-for-you

- **Sell the outcome of setup, not the hours.** Nobody buys ten steps; they buy not having to do them.
- **Never guarantee a go-live date.** ≤7 days is an internal target with no measured average.
- **Never imply a result from the build** — no lead volume, no conversion rate, no revenue. There is no data. See [what we cannot claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).
- Setup fees are explicitly non-refundable under the refund policy. Disclose, do not bury.
