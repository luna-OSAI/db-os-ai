# Subscription plans

**The live website is authoritative for these prices.** If the site changes, this file changes.

## The three plans

### Pro — $97/month
Entry access to the platform. Partner payout: **40% tier 1**.

### Partner — $297/month
Partner payout: **45% tier 1 + 5% tier 2**. The name signals the intent — this is the tier for
someone who plans to resell.

### Platinum — $497/month
Partner payout: **50% tier 1 + 30% tier 2 + 10% tier 3**. The full three-level payout structure.

## The 7-day free trial

Verified from the Refund Policy:

- The trial **automatically converts to a paid subscription** at day 7 unless cancelled in advance.
- The customer authorises the charge by starting the trial.
- The refund policy states that charge is **non-refundable, even if the platform is unused**.
- Trial eligibility is **one per customer or company** unless agreed in writing.

**Every promotion of the trial must state that it auto-converts and how to cancel.** Failing to
disclose that is the single most likely consumer-law problem in the whole offer. See
[ACL and consumer law](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/acl-and-consumer-law.md).

## What differentiates the tiers

**Not documented.** The only verified difference between $97, $297 and $497 is the partner payout
percentage. Whether platform capability, usage limits, support level or included builds differ is
recorded nowhere in the source set.

**Copy must not invent a feature difference between tiers.** If a prospect asks what they get at
Platinum that they do not get at Pro, the honest answer is that it needs confirming — and it goes
on the roadmap as gap G12. This is a real hole in a live pricing page and worth closing quickly.

## Billing posture

- Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.
- **All purchases are stated as final**, including subscription fees, converted trials, done-for-you services, setup and onboarding, and facilitated third-party purchases.
- No partial refunds for unused time, dissatisfaction or non-use.
- Dissatisfaction route is `success@teamos.ai` for investigation, support and optimisation.

**This posture cannot be stated in marketing as removing the buyer's rights.** Australian Consumer
Law guarantees cannot be contracted out of. See [ACL and consumer law](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/acl-and-consumer-law.md)
before writing any checkout, trial or refund copy.

## Writing about price

- **Lead with the comparison, not the number.** $297 against a HubSpot-class stack is a strong argument; $297 in isolation is just a price. But the HubSpot figure needs a live source — see [competitors](../06-research-and-market/competitor-hubspot.md).
- **Never present a tier difference you cannot name.**
- **Never imply the trial is risk-free** without stating the auto-conversion.
- Currency is not specified on the site. Say "per month" and avoid asserting USD or AUD until confirmed.
