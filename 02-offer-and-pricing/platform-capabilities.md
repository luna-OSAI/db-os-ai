# Platform capabilities

The capability surface, organised as twelve modules. **This is the file topic clusters draw their
"solution" section from** — every cluster links here rather than restating features.

> These capabilities are the GoHighLevel feature surface, white-labelled. Accurate as a description
> of what a customer gets. **Never attributed to GoHighLevel client-facing.**

## 1 · CRM and contact management
*Replaces: spreadsheets, HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce Essentials*

Unified CRM · structured contact notes with attachments and pinning · multi-object task linking
across contacts, companies, opportunities and custom objects · custom objects on all plans ·
AI business-card scanner · customisable kanban pipelines · SmartList filtering with saved layouts ·
lead scoring · coloured pipeline tags.

## 2 · Marketing and sales automation
*Replaces: Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, ClickFunnels, Calendly*

Email and SMS marketing with behaviour triggers · AI smart-send and send-time optimisation ·
email AI synced to your knowledge base · AI content import (forward a competitor's email, get an
editable template) · drip deliverability protection against carrier throttling · campaign
scheduler · trigger-based messaging · behavioural segmentation.

## 3 · Funnels and landing pages
*Replaces: ClickFunnels, Leadpages, Unbounce, Instapage*

Drag-and-drop builder · AI funnel and website generation from a prompt · A/B testing · 200+
templates · embedded booking · built-in SEO · AI schema markup generator (201+ schema types).

## 4 · Website builder
*Replaces: Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, a web developer*

Drag-and-drop, SEO-optimised, mobile responsive · AI site generation · eCommerce · membership
portals with tiered access and automated billing · WordPress integration and performance reports.

## 5 · Workflows and automation
*Replaces: Zapier, Make.com, manual process, hiring someone to connect everything*

Node-based visual builder · **workflow AI scaffolding** — describe an automation in plain English
and the logic skeleton is built · AI troubleshooter that flags broken steps and explains workflows
in plain English · advanced branching · company-level workflows for B2B · external form tracking on
non-OS forms · **missed-call triggers**.

## 6 · AI Agent Studio
*Replaces: hiring staff for repetitive sales, support and engagement*

No-code visual agent builder · specialised nodes (agent, router, knowledge base, web search, API,
MCP server, text/audio/image/video) · draft → staging → production with version control and
rollback · event-driven triggers on native CRM events · intent-based routing · multi-bot
architecture where bots tag-team mid-conversation · multiple knowledge bases per agent.

## 7 · Conversation AI — chat agents
*Replaces: Intercom, Drift, Zendesk Chat, after-hours missed leads*

Channels: SMS · web chat · Instagram DM · Facebook Messenger · Google Business Messages · TikTok DM · WhatsApp.

Instant multi-channel replies · in-conversation appointment booking with live calendar sync ·
autopilot or human-approval modes · trained on your FAQs, site and brand voice · web-crawler
training · multi-modal (interprets images and audio) · **chat widget voice AI** — visitors speak to
an agent on any page · configurable display name and compliance notices.

## 8 · Voice AI — phone agents
*Replaces: receptionists for routine calls, manual outbound, missed-call revenue leakage*

Voice AI 2.0 · outbound calling with DNC and permission checks · separate live and post-call action
paths · multilingual and accent-adaptive · **sentiment detection with escalation to a human** ·
real-time data lookups mid-call via webhooks.

## 9 · Communication hub

Unified inbox across SMS, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business, live chat and
TikTok. One dashboard, no tab-switching.

## 10 · Calendars and scheduling
*Replaces: Calendly, Acuity*

Booking pages, round-robin and team calendars, reminders, confirmations, no-show follow-up.

## 11 · Payments and commerce

Invoicing, estimates with status-triggered workflows, subscriptions, checkout, eCommerce.

## 12 · Courses, communities and memberships
*Replaces: Kajabi, Teachable, Skool*

Course delivery, gated membership tiers, community, automated billing.

## How to use this file in copy

- **Never list features.** Pick the one capability that answers the pain in front of you.
- **Always pair with what it replaces** — the replacement framing is the argument, the feature is just the mechanism.
- **Capability is not outcome.** "Books appointments 24/7" describes what it does. It is not a claim about how many appointments a specific customer will get.
