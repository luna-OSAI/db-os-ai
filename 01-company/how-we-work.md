# How we work

## The onboarding sequence

Ten steps, with named owners. This is the real documented process, not a marketing simplification.

| # | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm payment and kickoff prep | Tumai |
| 2 | Welcome, set up access, prepare kickoff call | Audrey |
| 3 | Create workspace and files | Audrey |
| 4 | Personalise CRM — stage 1 | — |
| 5 | Deliver kickoff call | — |
| 6 | Set up CRM core features — stage 2 | — |
| 7 | Connect social media and lead sources | — |
| 8 | Activate automations and go live | — |
| 9 | QA review and internal check | — |
| 10 | Finalise and mark complete | — |

**Step 2 in detail** — the client is added to the platform, given portal access, sent a welcome
email with a kickoff booking link, and assigned an account manager. Before the kickoff call, the
team reviews the client's **top three problems** and prepares a tailored strategy for that specific
client.

That last detail is the one worth using in copy. The kickoff is prepared against the client's
actual problems, not run from a script.

**Target onboarding time is ≤7 days.** *(Second Brain KPI list — a target, not a measured average.)*

## The automation library

OS A.I maintains a built inventory of workflows across five departments. This is evidence of
delivery capability — these are named, existing automations, not a roadmap.

| Department | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Marketing** | Get more leads | New leads · lead gen & qualification · funnel opt-in · IG comments and DMs · four lead magnets · email and SMS sequences · long-term nurture · stale-lead reactivation · cold email campaigns (case study, pain point, quick question, scarcity variants) · engagement scoring · marketing diagnostic quiz |
| **02 Sales** | Make more sales | Discovery call booking · high-ticket demo calendar · AI voice inbound and outbound · call-back booking · appointment confirmation and reminders · no-show follow-up · checkout and payments |
| **03 Operations** | Keep more clients | New client onboarding · kickoff call · payments · retention and growth · review requests · course sales and management · community and Skool onboarding |
| **04 Partners** | Create more referrals | Partner creation · partner activation · partner sales tracking |
| **05 Admin** | Support infrastructure | Partners OS website + AI · AI chat · AI voice · maintenance |

Note the departments map exactly onto the three outcomes: **Marketing → leads · Sales → sales ·
Operations → retention.** The structure is the argument.

## What this means for content

- **The process is documented and owned.** That is genuinely uncommon at this size and is worth saying.
- **"Done-for-you" is literal.** Ten steps, named owners, QA gate before completion.
- **Do not promise a timeline as a guarantee.** ≤7 days is an internal target with no measured average behind it. Phrase as intent, never as a commitment. See [claims policy](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/claims-policy.md).
