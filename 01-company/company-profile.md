## What the company does

The binding description, from the Terms and Conditions:

> "We help businesses become A.I enabled and ready by building custom bots, systems and automations
> to scale into the future."

That sentence is the safest description available, because it is contractual. Everything else is
marketing framing on top of it.

**In plainer terms:** OS A.I sells an all-in-one business platform — CRM, marketing automation,
funnels, websites, courses, communities — fronted by AI chat and voice agents that capture, qualify
and book leads without a human present. It is sold as a monthly subscription, and separately as a
done-for-you build where OS A.I installs the client's system for them.

## What it is built on

## Vision and mission

**Vision** — to become the global standard for AI-powered business automation, empowering 10,000+
small to medium businesses by 2027 to grow 10× faster while reclaiming 15+ hours per week.
*(Business plan, 2026-03-22. An internal goal, not a description of the present.)*

**Mission** — to take the complexity out of running a business: smart AI systems that handle the
busy work, free up time, and let owners focus on growing, creating impact and enjoying life.

**Brand promise** — **"The Magic Is In The System."**

**The three outcomes** the whole business organises around:

1. **Get More Leads**
2. **Make More Sales**
3. **Keep More Customers**

Every asset should be traceable to one of the three. If it isn't, it is off-strategy.

## Stage and honest position

- Revenue target of **$18,000 combined MRR** was set for end-2026, scaling to $200,000 MRR by end-2027. These are goals. **No actual revenue figure exists in any source.**
- Every "Current /m" metric field in the sales plan reads `[0000]` — **no performance baseline has been recorded.**
- **No case study, testimonial or named client** appears anywhere in the source material.
- The team is five people. See [leadership](leadership.md).

What this means for content: OS A.I competes on **the argument and the system**, not on a track
