# FAQ — canonical answers

**These answers are authoritative. Paraphrase freely for a differently-worded question; never change
the substance or the compliance posture.**

The point of a canonical FAQ is that the business never answers the same question two different ways.

## About the product

**What is OS A.I?**
An all-in-one platform — CRM, email and SMS, funnels, websites, calendars, payments, courses and
community — with AI chat and voice agents on the front of it that answer enquiries, qualify people
and book appointments around the clock.

**What does it replace?**
Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, ClickFunnels, Calendly, Zapier, Intercom, Wix, Kajabi and similar. Full
map: [what we replace](../02-offer-and-pricing/what-we-replace.md).

**What's the platform built on?**
> "We license and configure enterprise infrastructure rather than rebuilding what already works.
> What you're buying is the system we build on top of it — the agents, the automations, the setup
> and the support."
>
> **Never name GoHighLevel. Never claim to have built the platform.** [White-label disclosure](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/white-label-disclosure.md).

**Who are Oliver and Olivia?**
The AI agents on the website. **They must never claim to be human**, and should say they're AI if
asked. [Chat agent spec](chat-agent-spec.md).

**Will it sound robotic?**
It's trained on your material and can be set so you approve messages before they send. It also
detects when someone's frustrated and hands them to a person.

## Pricing

**What does it cost?**
Three plans: **Pro $97/month, Partner $297/month, Platinum $497/month.**

**What's the difference between the plans?**
> **The documented difference is the partner commission rate** — 40%, 45%+5%, and 50%+30%+10%.
> **Anything beyond that is not documented.** Say you'll confirm it. **Never invent a feature
> difference** — gap G12.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes, seven days. **It converts to a paid subscription at the end unless you cancel first** — always
say this. Trial eligibility is one per customer or company.

**Do you do refunds?**
The policy is that purchases are final, including setup fees and converted trials. If something's
not working, contact success@teamos.ai and we'll investigate and help.
> **Never say or imply the customer has no rights.** Australian Consumer Law guarantees can't be
> contracted out of. [ACL and consumer law](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/acl-and-consumer-law.md).

**What does the done-for-you build cost?**
$2,500–$11,100 setup depending on chat or voice and tier, plus the monthly.
> ⚠️ Business-plan pricing, not published. **Confirm before quoting.**

## Getting started

**How long does setup take?**
We target under a week. **Never promise a date** — it depends on the setup. [How we work](../01-company/how-we-work.md).

**What happens during onboarding?**
Ten steps with named owners: access and workspace, CRM configuration in two stages, a kickoff call
prepared around your top three problems, connecting your lead sources, activating automations, a QA
review, then sign-off.

**Do I have to migrate my data myself?**
No — that's part of the setup.

**Do I need to be technical?**
No. **Never say "it's easy"** — banned. The honest answer is that the setup is the hard part, which
is why it's done for you.
