# Objections — the SMB owner

> **`inferred`.** These are the objections the team expects, not objections captured from real
> calls. **Record five discovery calls and this file gets materially better.**

## The two real ones

Almost every stated objection is a proxy for one of these:

### "I don't believe this will actually get set up"

**Behind:** "too expensive" · "I need to think about it" · "send me some information" · "let me
discuss it with my partner"

**Why it's there:** he has bought a platform before, never finished configuring it, and is probably
still paying for it. This is fear 2 in his profile — *implementing the wrong tech* — and it is the
real blocker.

**The answer:**
> "That's the most common reason this doesn't work, and it's why the setup is the product rather
> than a bonus. Ten steps, each with someone's name against it, and a QA review before anyone calls
> it done. You're not being handed software to figure out."

**Do not answer this with more capability.** Every feature you add sounds like more work.

### "I don't want to depend on something I don't understand"

**Behind:** "how does the AI work?" · "what if it says the wrong thing?" · "I'd rather do it myself"
· "my customers want a person"

**Why it's there:** he wants time back **without losing control.** That is his stated want, and
automation copy that sounds like removing him from his business triggers the fear instead.

**The answer:**
> "Then stay in the loop. Approval mode means nothing goes out without you seeing it. You can move
> to full auto for the parts you're comfortable with and keep your hands on the rest."

## Stated objections

### "It's too expensive"

First, find out what it is being compared to. Usually it is being compared to nothing.

> "Compared to what you're paying now across everything, or compared to doing nothing? Both are fair
> — they're just different conversations."

Then the comparison, if relevant: HubSpot Professional is $890/month plus a $3,000 onboarding fee.
⚠️ Tier 2.

**Never** discount to close. **Never** invent an ROI figure.

### "I don't have time to set this up"

> "You don't set it up. That's what the setup fee is for. What we need from you is about two hours
> across the first week — the kickoff call and answering questions about how you actually work."

### "I've tried an all-in-one before and it didn't work"

**The truest objection in the set.** Do not defend the category.

> "What happened? …Yeah. That's the normal outcome, and it's not a discipline failure — you were sold
> software and left to implement a project. Which part never got finished?"

Let him tell the story. It sells the done-for-you line better than any pitch.

### "My customers want to talk to a real person"

> "For the conversation that matters, absolutely. Not for 'are you open Saturday?' The agent handles
> the questions that don't need you and books the ones that do."

### "How many clients do you have? / Do you have case studies?"

> "We're early. I'd rather show you the system than quote you a number, and there aren't published
> case studies yet."

**Never invent one.** An owner in this market recognises fabricated numbers, and honesty here
converts better than a statistic that unravels in month two. [What we cannot claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).

### "I need to think about it"

Usually the setup fear. Do not push.

> "Of course. Can I ask what you'd be weighing up? If it's whether it'll actually get implemented,
> that's worth talking through now rather than you deciding on your own."

### "Let me talk to my business partner"

> "Makes sense. What'll they want to know that I haven't covered? I'd rather you had the answer than
> have to guess at it."

### "What's the difference between Pro and Platinum?"

> **The documented difference is the partner commission rate. Anything else is not documented.**
> "The commission structure differs — let me confirm the rest rather than guess."
> **Never invent it.** Gap G12.

### "Can you guarantee this will work?"

> "No, and I wouldn't trust anyone who did. What I can do is show you exactly what gets built and
> how it works."

## Never

- Any invented result, client count or testimonial
- Any guarantee
- Discounting to close
- "Can you afford not to?"
- "You could make it back by referring people" — **ACL s49**
- Manufactured urgency
