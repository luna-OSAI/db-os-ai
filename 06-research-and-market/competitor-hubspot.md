# Competitor — HubSpot

The comparison OS A.I reaches for most often. **This file unblocks the "$890/month" claim** that had
been circulating across the internal documents with no source attached.

## Verified pricing — HubSpot Marketing Hub

Source: HubSpot's own pricing page, researched 2026-08-18.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Seats included | Onboarding fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 2 users | — |
| Starter | $7/seat | $20/seat | per seat | — |
| **Professional** | **$800/mo** | **$890/mo** | 3 core seats | **$3,000 one-time** |
| Enterprise | $3,600/mo min. | — | 5 core seats | **$7,000 one-time** |

Additional seats: $45/mo (Professional), $75/mo (Enterprise). Professional includes 2,000 marketing
contacts; Enterprise 10,000. Contacts above the tier cost more.

## The $890 finding

**The figure repeated throughout the OS A.I source material is correct** — it is HubSpot Marketing
Hub Professional on annual billing. It had simply never been sourced or dated, which is why it was
blocked in the [claims register](../05-proof-and-evidence/claims-register.md).

**It is now usable, with two conditions:**

1. **Always attribute and date it** — "HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional, $890/month on annual billing, as published August 2026."
2. **Always name the plan.** "HubSpot costs $890" is misleading — HubSpot starts at $0 and their Starter tier is $20/seat. The honest comparison is Professional, which is the tier an SMB gets pushed to for automation and reporting.

**The stronger, more honest version of the argument includes the onboarding fee.** HubSpot
Professional requires a mandatory **$3,000 one-time onboarding payment**. That is the detail that
actually makes OS A.I's point — the platform is not the whole cost, setup is — and it comes from
HubSpot's own page.

## Where HubSpot is genuinely strong

Say this. Pretending otherwise fails against an agency founder who knows the market.

- Deep, mature product with a large ecosystem and integration library
- Excellent free tier — a real on-ramp
- Strong reporting and attribution
- Enormous content and education presence
- Enterprise-grade credibility: security posture, procurement, SSO, compliance documentation

**OS A.I has none of the last point documented.** Do not compete there.

## Where the comparison favours OS A.I

- **Price at the automation tier.** $890/mo + $3,000 onboarding versus $497/mo at the top OS A.I tier.
- **Seat-based pricing punishes growth.** Adding a fifth person adds $45/mo, indefinitely.
- **Contact-tier escalation** — the bill grows with the list, not with the value.
- **Setup is the customer's problem** — or a $3,000 line item. OS A.I's done-for-you line is a direct answer.
- **Voice agents are not a HubSpot product.**

## How to use it

- **Never lead with the competitor.** Lead with the reader's problem.
- **Compare tier to tier**, name the plan, quote the source and the date.
- **Include the onboarding fee** — it is the most telling number on the page.
- **Never characterise HubSpot as bad.** It is good and expensive. That is the argument.
- Re-verify before any published use. Pricing changes.
