# Market context — Australia

> **`draft`.** No primary Australian market research has been conducted. What is verified here is
> the **regulatory** context; the market sizing and adoption commentary is reasoning, not data.

## Why Australia is the primary market

- The company is Australian — ABN 75 527 278 804, Werribee VIC
- Both founders and three of five staff are AU-based
- The ICP is defined as AU/NZ/North America, with AU first
- Australian English and local idiom are an explicit voice rule
- Australian ownership is a genuine trust asset in a category full of anonymous offshore resellers

## The regulatory environment — verified

This part is solid and it shapes everything OS A.I publishes. Full detail in
[ACL and consumer law](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/acl-and-consumer-law.md).

| Regime | Effect on OS A.I |
|---|---|
| **Australian Consumer Law** (Competition and Consumer Act 2010 Sch 2) | s18 misleading conduct · s29 false representations · consumer guarantees that cannot be contracted out of |
| **Spam Act 2003** | Consent, sender identification and functional unsubscribe on every email and SMS — including AI-sent messages |
| **Do Not Call Register Act 2006** | DNC checks before outbound voice |
| **Privacy Act 1988 / APPs** | Collection notices, purpose limitation, secure handling |
| **ACL Part 3-1 Div 3 and s49** | Pyramid scheme and referral selling prohibitions — bear directly on the partner program |

**This is a competitive asset, not just a constraint.** An AU-based operator who understands Spam
Act and DNC obligations is meaningfully safer for an Australian buyer than an offshore vendor who
does not — and most of the category does not talk about this at all. Compliance literacy is a
differentiator OS A.I is not currently using.

## The market, reasoned rather than researched

Directionally true, not evidenced:

- Australian SMBs are broadly later to marketing-automation adoption than US equivalents
- Price sensitivity is higher — US-priced SaaS lands harder against AUD revenue
- Trades and professional services are strongly represented in the target profile
- Word of mouth carries disproportionate weight in local service markets
- **AI adoption is at the "should we?" → "how?" transition**, which is the moment the business plan identifies as its opportunity
