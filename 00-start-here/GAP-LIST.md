# Gap list

What this database is being asked to produce, against what the sources can actually support.
**This is the agenda for the interview round (S2), ordered by how much it blocks.**

---

## Blocking — an asset cannot be written honestly without this

### G1 · Which offer is real, today?

Three incompatible offer structures exist (see the source register). Nothing can be written about
price, packaging or what a buyer gets until this resolves. **Proposed default:** the live website
is authoritative for the subscription; the business-plan setup fees describe a separate
done-for-you services line. Needs confirmation, not assumption.

### G2 · There is no proof of anything

No testimonials. No case studies. No client count. No documented result. Meanwhile the live site
publishes **"Scale Your Business 10× Faster"**, **"Join 100s of entrepreneurs"**,
**"$50,000+ pre-built business kit"**, **"We cover $5,000/m in plugins"** and
**"Loved By All Industries — All Over The World."**

Under Australian Consumer Law, s18 and s29, a business must be able to substantiate performance
claims. **Not one of those five claims is supported by any document in the source set.** Either
evidence exists somewhere not supplied, or the site is currently making unsubstantiated claims.
**Resolved by decision, not by evidence.** Owner decision D2 (2026-08-18) permits reuse of the five
claims as published, on the basis that more information is coming. That is a legitimate business
call and it is recorded transparently — but **it settles what copy may say, not whether the claims
are substantiated.** The evidence gap remains open and is item 2 in the
[enrichment roadmap](ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md).

A sixth site claim — "Save hundreds of hours and thousands of dollars per month" — was **not** part
of D2 and is not approved for reuse.

### G3 · The partner program's compliance posture

The live site advertises three-tier commissions (40% tier 1 · 45% + 5% tier 2 · 50% + 30% + 10%
tier 3). Multi-level payout structures attract ACCC scrutiny around referral selling and pyramid
schemes, and any earnings implication triggers income-claim rules. There is an Affiliate Policy
document, but no statement of what a partner may and may not say when recruiting. Without this,
no partner-facing asset can be written.

### G4 · Refund posture versus Australian Consumer Law

The refund policy states "all purchases are final… no refunds will be issued," including for a
7-day trial that auto-converts. Consumer guarantees under the ACL cannot be contracted out of, and
the ACCC has previously acted against businesses advertising blanket no-refund terms. This does not
make the policy unusable, but it does mean **marketing copy must never state or imply that a buyer
has no refund rights.** Needs an explicit ruling before any checkout, trial or sales-page copy.

### G5 · No voice of customer, at all

Not one sentence of real prospect or customer language exists in the source set. "Entrepreneur
Ethan" is a well-constructed persona built from the team's assumptions — it is `inferred`, not
observed. Every hook, objection turn and article angle would inherit that inference. This is the
single highest-leverage gap in the whole build: one recorded sales call closes more of it than
another month of internal documents.

## Significant — the asset can be written, but weaker

### G6 · No competitor research exists

HubSpot is named and priced ("$890/month") repeatedly across the source set with **no source and no
date**. The business plan's competitor section is an unfilled template. Competitor teardowns must
be researched from scratch in S6, with URLs and `researched:` dates, or the comparison claims that
carry most of the marketing weight cannot be published.

### G7 · Brand name is unresolved in public

Three names are in play: **Oscale A.I** (legal entity, ABN 75 527 278 804), **OS A.I** (the brand
used throughout the documents and on-site), and **Team OS** (named as parent; owns `success@teamos.ai`).
Copy needs one rule for which name appears where.

### G8 · No design or brand asset specification

The archetype document prescribes purple / indigo / black / gold and specific typography as
*archetype theory*. Whether OS A.I actually uses those is unknown — no logo, palette or type spec
was supplied. Visual direction for any asset is currently guesswork.

### G9 · Systems and IDs are described but not enumerated

Onboarding references a GoHighLevel sub-account, Slack, Asana and a client portal, but no location
ID, calendar ID, pipeline names, form IDs or repo is recorded. Anything that must integrate with
live systems will need these.

### G10 · The AI agents are named but not specified

The site fronts **"Oliver"** and **"Olivia."** Nothing in the source set defines their personality,
scope, escalation rules or what they must never say — despite them being the primary sales surface.

## Worth knowing — shapes quality, does not block

- **G11 · No disqualifiers.** Who OS A.I turns away is never stated. Every good ICP file needs this.
- **G12 · No pricing rationale.** Why $97 / $297 / $497, and what actually differs between tiers beyond payout percentage.
- **G13 · No churn or retention data.** Retention targets (90–95%) exist as goals; no actual figure.
- **G14 · No SEO baseline.** No keyword research, no ranking data, no analytics for the article-brief engine to aim at.
- **G15 · No stated position on AI disclosure** — whether customers are told they are talking to an AI agent, which several jurisdictions increasingly require.

## What is already strong

Worth naming, because it is unusual for an intake to have this much:

- **Product capability is exceptionally well documented.** The platform feature reference is thorough and maps feature → benefit → what-it-replaces, which is precisely the shape topic clusters need.
- **The automation inventory is real** — 5 departments of named, built workflows. This is evidence of delivery capability, not aspiration.
- **The brand decision is made and reasoned.** 70% Magician / 30% Ruler, "The Magic Is In The System." That is a genuine company decision with a documented rationale.
- **The ICP is detailed**, even if inferred — demographics, psychographics, fears and aspirations for two distinct segments.
- **The hard rules are already explicit.** Never name GoHighLevel client-facing. Australian English. No-BS wizard. These came from the company, not from me.
