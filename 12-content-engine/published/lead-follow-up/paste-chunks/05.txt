<p>Avoid sending the same &ldquo;just following up&rdquo; message five times. Each touchpoint should give the prospect a reason to respond, whether that is an answer, a useful detail or an easier way to book.</p>

<h2 id="faq" class="lf-rev">Frequently Asked Questions</h2><div class="lf-faq">
<details><summary>What is automated lead follow-up?</summary><div class="lf-faq-body"><p>Automated lead follow-up is a set of workflows that respond to new enquiries and keep in touch with them without manual effort. It typically covers an instant first reply, qualification questions, booking links and a timed sequence of follow-up messages.</p></div></details>
<details><summary>How quickly should you follow up with a new lead?</summary><div class="lf-faq-body"><p>As quickly as you can, ideally within minutes. Interest is usually highest at the moment someone enquires, so a fast first reply gives you the best chance of starting the conversation. Automation makes that possible after hours and on weekends, when nobody is at a desk.</p></div></details>
<details><summary>How many follow-up messages should you send?</summary><div class="lf-faq-body"><p>Most businesses do well with four to five touchpoints over about a week, as in the sequence above. Vary the content each time and stop the moment the lead books or asks you to stop.</p></div></details>
<details><summary>Do you need a CRM to automate lead follow-up?</summary><div class="lf-faq-body"><p>You need one place where lead data lives, and a CRM is the usual answer. Without it, automation has no reliable record of who enquired, what they asked and where they are in the journey.</p></div></details>
<details><summary>Will automated follow-up feel impersonal?</summary><div class="lf-faq-body"><p>It does not have to. Automate the predictable steps, such as the first reply, common questions and reminders, and hand over to a person for anything complex or sensitive. Writing the messages in your own tone helps as well.</p></div></details>
<details><summary>What should you test before launching lead follow-up automation?</summary><div class="lf-faq-body"><p>Submit a test enquiry and check the whole journey: contact creation, message timing, personalisation fields, qualification logic, calendar links, team notifications and whether a booking stops the sequence. Then test what happens when a lead never replies, books straight away or is not qualified.</p></div></details>
