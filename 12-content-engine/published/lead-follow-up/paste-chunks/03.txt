<p id="lf-start">Most businesses do the opposite. They switch on a tool first and decide what it should do later, which leaves leads slipping through gaps nobody planned for. This guide gives you the order that works, so the system handles the repetitive work and your team spends its time on the conversations that need a person.</p>
<div class="lf-brief lf-rev" id="tldr"><div class="lf-panel-bar"></div><div class="lf-panel-in"><h2>TL;DR</h2><p class="lf-brief-note">The five steps in one glance</p><ul>
<li><a href="#step-1"><strong>Step 1:</strong> Map the path from first enquiry to booked appointment before you build anything.</a></li>
<li><a href="#step-2"><strong>Step 2:</strong> Keep every lead in one CRM with clear ownership and only the pipeline stages you use.</a></li>
<li><a href="#step-3"><strong>Step 3:</strong> Automate the first response so every enquiry gets a reply straight away, including after hours.</a></li>
<li><a href="#step-4"><strong>Step 4:</strong> Add a short follow-up sequence over about a week, with a different reason to reply each time.</a></li>
<li><a href="#step-5"><strong>Step 5:</strong> Test the full journey, including the edge cases, before you go live.</a></li>
</ul></div></div>
