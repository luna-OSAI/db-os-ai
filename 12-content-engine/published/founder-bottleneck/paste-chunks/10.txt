<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Add the scores, then identify the three highest areas. Those are your first systemisation targets.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">As a rough guide rather than a measurement: any single area scoring 4 or 5 is a process that has quietly become a person. A total above 35 usually means the issue is structural across the business rather than isolated to one bad area.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">You do not need to rebuild everything. You need to remove the biggest constraints first.</p>
<h2 id="mistakes" style="font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-weight:700 !important;font-size:clamp(30px,4vw,44px) !important;line-height:1.06 !important;letter-spacing:-.003em !important;color:#002147 !important;margin:80px 0 24px !important;padding-top:24px !important;border-top:1px solid #DDE5EE !important;scroll-margin-top:90px !important">Common mistakes when removing the founder bottleneck</h2>
<h3 style="font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-weight:700 !important;font-size:clamp(21px,2.4vw,26px) !important;line-height:1.18 !important;color:#002147 !important;margin:40px 0 16px !important">Hiring before building the process</h3>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">A new hire cannot reliably own a process that only exists inside the founder's head. Document the workflow first, then give someone ownership of it.</p>
<h3 style="font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-weight:700 !important;font-size:clamp(21px,2.4vw,26px) !important;line-height:1.18 !important;color:#002147 !important;margin:40px 0 16px !important">Automating everything at once</h3>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Automation is a means, not the goal. A business with 50 complicated automations nobody understands is harder to operate than one with five clear workflows. Start with high-volume, predictable processes.</p>
<h3 style="font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-weight:700 !important;font-size:clamp(21px,2.4vw,26px) !important;line-height:1.18 !important;color:#002147 !important;margin:40px 0 16px !important">Delegating tasks without authority</h3>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">If someone is responsible for a task but cannot make the decisions required to complete it, the task will keep returning to you. Give people defined decision rights.</p>
<h3 style="font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-weight:700 !important;font-size:clamp(21px,2.4vw,26px) !important;line-height:1.18 !important;color:#002147 !important;margin:40px 0 16px !important">Keeping every exception</h3>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Few exceptions genuinely need founder involvement. Define what actually qualifies as an escalation, or "just checking" becomes the default operating system.</p>
<h3 style="font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-weight:700 !important;font-size:clamp(21px,2.4vw,26px) !important;line-height:1.18 !important;color:#002147 !important;margin:40px 0 16px !important">Building systems nobody uses</h3>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">A documented process only helps if the team follows it. Keep workflows visible, simple, and connected to the tools people already use.</p>

<h2 style="font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-weight:700 !important;font-size:clamp(24px,3vw,33px) !important;line-height:1.1 !important;margin:0 0 16px !important;padding:0 !important;border:0 !important;color:#002147 !important">What this does not solve</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 14px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Systemising the founder bottleneck fixes a capacity problem. It does not fix a demand problem. If the real issue is that not enough enquiries arrive, faster follow-up on a thin pipeline will show you that more clearly rather than change it. It does not fix an offer the market does not want, and it will not rescue pricing that never worked.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 14px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">It also does not touch the keep list from Step 2. Those decisions stay yours, and the time you free up is meant to go there.</p>
<p style="margin:0 !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">And it will not work on a business where nobody is willing to let a decision be made differently from how you would have made it. If every call your team makes without you gets reversed, the authority is decorative and the escalations will come straight back. That part is not a systems problem.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">OS A.I connects the workflows that keep a business moving, so more of the routine operation runs without the founder pushing each step forward.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 14px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">That covers AI chat agents, voice agents, CRM processes and automation working from one system rather than several disconnected ones. Approval mode keeps you in the loop on anything you want to see before it goes out.</p>
<p style="margin:0 !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">The founder stays where judgement matters. The system handles what repeats. The team owns what has been clearly defined.</p>
