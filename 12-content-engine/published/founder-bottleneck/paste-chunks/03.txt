<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Those companies were not lazy. They were busy, and the response depended on someone who was doing something else.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Hiring more people can increase the amount of work being produced. But if every person still needs the founder to make the final call, founder dependency survives the hire and you have added management to your week.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Technology creates the same trap. Adding another CRM, project management platform, form or automation does not remove founder dependency on its own. If the underlying process still says "ask the founder", you have moved the bottleneck into a new tool.</p>
<p style="margin:32px 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:clamp(21px,2.5vw,27px) !important;line-height:1.32 !important;font-weight:600 !important;color:#002147 !important;border-left:3px solid #007FFF !important;padding:2px 0 2px 20px !important">Build a business where the system carries the repeatable work, your team owns defined outcomes, and you stay focused on decisions that genuinely require you.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Start by looking at the last two weeks of your business. Do not start by asking, "What should I delegate?" Ask: <strong style="color:#002147 !important;font-weight:700 !important">"What keeps coming back to me?"</strong></p>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Look through email, CRM notifications, Slack or Teams messages, missed calls and voicemail, project tasks, customer escalations, sales opportunities, approvals, meetings, recurring reports and manual admin.</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px !important;font-family:'Barlow Condensed',sans-serif !important;font-size:18px !important;line-height:1.62 !important;color:#2D2D2D !important">Write down every recurring request that needed your attention. Then group them.</p>
