## Where the opportunity probably is

Reasoned, not measured:

1. **"HubSpot pricing" / "HubSpot alternative"** — high commercial intent, and OS A.I now has genuinely sourced pricing including the $3,000 onboarding fee that most comparison content omits. **The strongest starting point.**
2. **"AI agent vs chatbot"** — definitional queries are winnable with a clear, honest explainer, and this one has real confusion behind it.
3. **Long-tail question queries** — "what should I automate first", "why doesn't my team use the CRM". Low volume, extremely high intent, and almost nobody answers them well.
4. **Australian modifiers** — "for Australian businesses", "australia". Smaller pool, far less competition, and OS A.I is genuinely local.

## Where not to compete

- **"AI for small business"** unqualified — dominated by enormous publishers
- **"CRM"** unqualified — HubSpot and Salesforce own it
- **"Business automation"** unqualified — saturated

**Go narrow and specific.** A page that answers one real question beats a page targeting a broad
term it will never rank for.
