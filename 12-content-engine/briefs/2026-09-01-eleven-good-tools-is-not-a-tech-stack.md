## 2 · Why this category, and why this angle

The ask was the topic most Australians would search. Ranked honestly:

| AI Agents | Highest | Low — enormous publishers own it | **Twice** |

| Follow-Up · Workflows · Setup · Founder Bottleneck | Low | High | Yes |

Tech Stack is the only tab both **unwritten** and **defensible**. The keyword map already says to go
narrow rather than compete on "CRM" or "AI for small business" unqualified, and to use Australian
modifiers where the pool is smaller and the competition thinner.

## 3 · The argument

**One sentence:**

> Australian businesses spent three years adding tools and switching on AI while the share that
> actively collects and analyses its own data fell to one in ten, because the customer is spread
> across a stack where no single system holds the whole record.

**The reframe:** he believes his stack problem is the monthly bill, so he shops for a cheaper tool.
He should believe the bill is the smallest part, and that the real cost is owning nine partial
records of the same customer with nothing joining them, which is why none of his tools can answer a
question about his own business.

## 6 · What this piece must NOT claim

- No customer results, case studies or client counts. None exist
- No savings total, and **no arithmetic** built on the `inferred` $500–1,000 or 50–70% figures
- No timeline for a migration
- No claim that any named tool is bad. Calendly and Mailchimp are excellent; eleven excellent tools is still not a system
- **GoHighLevel never named**, no provenance implied
- The fall in data analysis must not be presented as proof that businesses got worse at analysis. The Business Characteristics Survey was redeveloped for this cycle. Report the level, report the fall as the ABS reports it, and name the redevelopment

## 8 · Objections to pre-empt

- "My tools work fine" → then this is not for you. When did you last add up the total?
- "All-in-one means mediocre at everything" → for a 1–10 person service business, a connected 8/10 beats five disconnected 10/10s
- "I do not want to migrate" → you do not do the migration
- "I tried an all-in-one and never finished setting it up" → the truest objection in the set, and the reason the setup is the product

> **Comparability note — step 4 of "Adding a stat".** The Business Characteristics Survey was
> redeveloped for this cycle: the previously alternating Innovation and Digital Activity modules were
> combined into one integrated framework on a biennial cycle. The ABS publishes the ICT

> reference period changed from two years to one. **Cite the 2024–25 levels as levels.** Where the

> passage. Do not describe it as businesses becoming worse at analysis.
>

> - Attribute as **ABS**, with the **2024–25** reference period and the **25 June 2026** release date, in the sentence.
