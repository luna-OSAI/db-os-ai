## 3 · The argument

**One sentence.**

> A CRM that requires discipline has already failed by design, because it asks for manual input now in exchange for a benefit that arrives later and to someone else — so the only CRM that stays current is one where the record is a by-product of the work.

**The reframe:** *"I'm bad at keeping the CRM updated"* → *"the CRM was built to need a maintainer, and I have been volunteering."*

## 8 · Objections to pre-empt

From [cluster-crm](../../11-topic-clusters/cluster-crm.md) §5 and [objections-smb](../../10-faq-and-objections/objections-smb.md):

- **"It'll be another thing nobody updates."** The real objection. Answered by the mechanism section — it stays current because the conversation, the booking and the payment already happen there
- **"My spreadsheet works fine."** Conceded in the opening rather than argued with; the piece never attacks the spreadsheet
- **"We're too small for a CRM."** Answered implicitly — the piece argues for a record that costs nothing to maintain, not for a formal CRM practice
- **"Moving my data sounds painful."** Named in the honest-limit section as real work, and as the reason the done-for-you line exists
